Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India

Introduction

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programs.

Biography

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He studied aerospace engineering and worked with India's space and defence research organizations.

He became the 11th President of India in 2002. He was widely known for his interaction with students and his vision for India's future.

Major Achievements

  • Worked with ISRO and DRDO.
  • Played an important role in India's missile development programs.
  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Received the Bharat Ratna.
  • Inspired millions of students across India.
